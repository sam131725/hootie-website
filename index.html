def twoSum(nums: list[int], target: int) -> list[int]:
    # Store complement and its index
    seen = {}
    for i, num in enumerate(nums):
        diff = target - num
        if diff in seen:
            return [seen[diff], i]
        seen[num] = i
    return []

    • Download the Windows installer (or the portable .exe if you can't install apps).
    • If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
    • Finish the installer and open Hootie from the Start menu.
    • The Chrome Web Store listing is coming soon. Until then, use the desktop app.
    • Once it's live: click Add to Chrome, pin the owl, and open it during a Google Meet call.

    💡 Yes, you can paste more than one key

    Put one key per line (commas work too). Hootie uses the first key, and if it hits a rate limit, runs out of credit or stops working, it switches to the next one automatically, mid-interview, without stopping.

    • The badge shows which key is active, e.g. Active key: 2 / 3.
    • "All keys exhausted" means it's time to top up or add a fresh key.
    • All keys in the box should be for the provider you picked. The same works for Groq keys.
    • Use keys you own (like a personal and a backup key). Don't create extra accounts just to get around free limits: providers don't allow it.

    • Click 🎙 Capture audio. The status turns red: Hootie is listening.

    Ethics

    Practise, don't pretend

    Hootie is built to make you better at interviews, not to fake your way through one. Here's how we think about using it responsibly.

    ✓ Great ways to use Hootie

    • Mock interviews and practice sessions on your own
    • Reviewing your answers afterwards and learning the frameworks
    • Calls where everyone has been told and has agreed
    • Building real confidence you take into the room yourself

    ✕ Please don't

    • Use it in a real interview or exam where AI help isn't allowed
    • Record or transcribe people without their permission
    • Repeat answers you don't understand as if they were your own
    • Use it to mislead an employer about your skills
    🤝 Consent firstHootie asks you to confirm everyone on the call has agreed before it starts listening.
    📸 Screen on requestYour screen is only read when you click Context. Nothing runs in the background.
    ⚖️ Know the rulesRecording laws differ by country and state, and many employers ban AI help in interviews. Check before you use it in any call.

    Why it matters: an answer you can't explain falls apart at the first follow-up question, and a job you got by pretending is a hard job to keep. The goal is for you to not need Hootie on the day.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    What is Hootie?

    Hootie is a real-time AI mock interview tool. It listens as you answer, recognises questions, and gives you live coaching and answer outlines so you can practise interviews realistically.

    How is Hootie different from other AI interview tools?

    Hootie works in real time on your own device. It's resume-aware, supports English, Hindi and Hinglish, and lets you bring your own AI key, so you can start free.

    Which platforms does it work on?

    There's a desktop app for macOS and Windows, and a Chrome extension that works with Google Meet.

    Is my data private?

    Yes. Hootie has no accounts and no database, so we never see your sessions. Transcripts, answers and your resume stay on your computer, and questions go straight to the AI provider you choose using your own key.

    Is it okay to use Hootie in a real interview?

    Hootie is made for practice. Many companies don't allow AI help during interviews, and using it there can get an offer withdrawn. Use Hootie to prepare, then go in and answer on your own. If you're unsure, ask the recruiter what's allowed.

    Can I use Hootie in a call with other people?

    Only with everyone's permission. Hootie asks you to confirm that everyone on the call has agreed before it starts listening. Recording or transcribing people without consent may be illegal where you live, and many companies don't allow AI help during real interviews.

    Can I add more than one API key?

    Yes. Paste one key per line in Settings, for both your AI provider and Groq. If a key hits its limit or runs out of credit, Hootie automatically switches to the next one. See the setup guide.

    Does Hootie read my screen?

    Only when you click Context. Hootie captures your screen once, reads the text on your own computer, and adds it to the question so answers match what you're looking at. Nothing is recorded in the background.

    Do I need an OpenAI or Claude account?

    Yes. Paste an OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or Gemini key in Settings, plus a Groq key for audio transcription. Using your own key is what keeps your sessions private: they go straight to your AI provider, not through us.
